01

Accompaniment of Youth

Vibe Check: Step 1 of 5

1. Gratitude 🙏 (Blessing Drop)

Drop some thankfulness. Recall concrete blessings of your day—big or small. What made you smile? Who showed you love? Acknowledge it fully.

2. Petition 🕯️ (Ask for Clarity)

Ask the Spirit of Truth for complete transparency. Request the grace to inspect your heart, thoughts, and actions today without heavy self-judgment or filters.

3. Review 🔍 (Interior Vibe Check)

Hour by hour, replay your day. Note your reactions, conversations, and emotional changes. Where did you feel energy, hope, and connection (*Consolation*)? Where did you feel drained, fearful, or defensive (*Desolation*)?

4. Forgiveness 🤍 (Reset & Restore)

Acknowledge where you fell short, acted out of selfishness, or caused harm. Gently drop those failures into the infinite ocean of mercy. You are forgiven—breathe in the grace.

5. Resolve ⚡ (Look Ahead)

Look forward to tomorrow with hopeful anticipation. Plan one small, conscious choice where you can show more patience, kindness, or courage. Resolve to live intentionally.

The Four Soul Workouts ⚔️

Ignatius organized the Spiritual Exercises into four major weeks (stages). This represents an intense journey from spiritual detox into deep discipleship, sharing suffering, and ultimate love.

Level I: Detoxing Attachments & Accepting Grace

Unpack the background noise and habits blocking your soul. Audit your attachments: separate true freedom from the noise, and connect with the reality of absolute, infinite, and unconditional mercy.

Liturgical Vibe Check: Sacred Reading

  • Luke 15:11-32 — The Prodigal Son (Home-coming of mercy)
  • Isaiah 43:1-7 — "You are precious in my eyes, and I love you"
  • Psalm 139 — The God who searches and knows you completely

Soul Drill: The Colloquy

St. Ignatius recommends concluding meditations with a Colloquy—speaking to Jesus or Mary as one friend speaks to another. Talk honestly about your chains, and rest in grace.

Level II: The Calling — Journeying with the King

Contemplate the active life of Jesus. Study his teachings, his style, and his interactions with the poor. Ask for the grace "to know Him more intimately, to love Him more intensely, and to follow Him more closely."

Liturgical Vibe Check: Sacred Reading

  • Luke 1:26-38 — The Annunciation (The bold 'Yes' of Mary)
  • Mark 1:16-20 — The Call of the First Disciples
  • Matthew 5:1-12 — The Beatitudes (The charter of the Kingdom)

Soul Drill: Imaginative Contemplation

Enter the scripture scene with all five senses. Watch the crowd, hear the wind, touch the dust. Position yourself directly in the scene as an active participant.

Level III: The Crucible — Standing in Solidarity

Accompany Jesus through Gethsemane, betrayal, trials, and the Cross. Pray for the grace of genuine sorrow and tears. Practice standing in absolute solidarity with all who suffer and are marginalized in our modern world.

Liturgical Vibe Check: Sacred Reading

  • John 13:1-17 — Washing of the Feet (Service and humility)
  • Mark 14:32-42 — Gethsemane (Agony and surrender)
  • Luke 23:26-49 — The Crucifixion (Standing at the foot of the cross)

Soul Drill: Bearing Grief

Observe how the divine hides itself during suffering, yet remains present. We pray for deep empathy for those who grieve and suffer under systemic injustice.

Level IV: The Sunrise — Rising and Living in Love

Swell with the pure joy of the Resurrection. End the Exercises with the famous *Contemplation to Attain Love*, realizing God is present in all created things, working tirelessly for us in the universe.

Liturgical Vibe Check: Sacred Reading

  • John 20:1-18 — The Resurrection (Meeting Mary Magdalene)
  • Luke 24:13-35 — Road to Emmaus (Recognizing Christ in the breaking of bread)
  • John 21:1-19 — Breakfast by the Sea ("Feed my sheep")

Soul Drill: Suscipe Prayer

Surrender your liberty back in gratitude: "Take, Lord, and receive all my liberty, my memory, my understanding, and my entire will. You have given them to me; to You I return them..."
